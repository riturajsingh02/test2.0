Our candles are hand-poured with 100% pure botanical soy wax harvested from sustainable soybean crops. We never blend paraffin, chemical hardeners, or petroleum products, ensuring a soot-free burn that is safe for children and pets.

We provide Cash on Delivery (COD) across India for orders up to ₹999. You can pay with cash or directly via UPI QR code to the courier partner upon parcel handover.

Metro cities typically receive parcels within 2–3 business days. Non-metro and regional locations are delivered within 4–7 business days via our Bluedart and Delhivery courier integrations.

On your initial burn, let the candle burn for 2 to 3 hours until the entire surface melts into an even liquid wax pool to the edge of the glass. Always trim wicks to 1/4 inch before each burn.
